為什麼要「翻譯」？Why "translate"?

  1. 指責是保護殼：「你都…」「你每次…」通常包著受傷、害怕或孤單。Blame is a shell: "you always…" usually wraps hurt, fear or loneliness.
  2. 對方只聽得到殼：聽到攻擊的人會防禦，於是越吵越遠。People only hear the shell: someone who hears an attack defends, and the gap grows.
  3. 說出需要，對方才接得住：「我需要被重視」比「你都不在乎」更容易被回應。Naming the need is what can be answered: "I need to feel I matter" gets a response that "you don't care" never will.
